# Rachel Elz

I've worked in marketing for 13 years, including hiring and managing teams. Now I build small AI tools for the jobs marketers still do by hand every week.

Three of them are marketing tools: competitor-watch, review-themes and weekly-campaign-report. Each runs on sample data in a couple of minutes, without connecting an account or sharing anything from your company, and each report opens with the one thing to look at first, so whoever reads it on Monday knows where to start. A Python script does the counting and comparing, and Claude, through Claude Code, writes the short summary on top.

| Repo | The weekly job it takes over |
|---|---|
| [competitor-watch](https://github.com/rachelelz/competitor-watch) | Checking competitor sites for changed headlines, prices and calls to action |
| [review-themes](https://github.com/rachelelz/review-themes) | Reading through reviews to find the complaints that keep coming back |
| [weekly-campaign-report](https://github.com/rachelelz/weekly-campaign-report) | Turning Monday's ad platform exports into a report for your manager |
| [job-hunt-loop](https://github.com/rachelelz/job-hunt-loop) | Checking job postings against your own criteria, and tailoring a CV only for the ones that pass |

The three marketing tools each have a section on what went wrong while I built them and what I'd change next.
